About South African History Online

Our History

South African History Online (SAHO) was established in 1999 and registered as a Non Profit Organisation in June 2000. Over two decades, SAHO has grown to become the largest and most comprehensive online resource on South African and African history and culture.

SAHO attracts approximately 2 million visitors annually and serves as a trusted resource for learners, educators, media, and the general public. In 2018, SAHO's founder, Omar Badsha, received the National Order of Mapungubwe (Silver) for preserving our country's heritage.

Mission, Vision and Values

Our Mission: SAHO is to break the silence on our past and develop a knowledgeable, broad-based representation of African and South African heritage in educational and cultural institutions.

Our Vision: To provide the study, teaching, and learning of history and to make knowledge accessible through the creation of a comprehensive online encyclopedia.

Our Values: Promoting a critical and rational understanding of our past, encouraging history education, and developing technologies to preserve history.

Our Founder & CEO

Omar Badsha
Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Omar Badsha, born in Durban in 1945, is an artist, writer, and a committed activist in the struggle for a just and non-racial South Africa. In 2018, he received the National Order of Mapungubwe (Silver) from the President of South Africa.
