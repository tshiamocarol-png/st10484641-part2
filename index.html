Towards a People's History

Welcome to South African History Online (SAHO). We are a non-partisan, non-profit organisation dedicated to breaking the silence on our past. Our mission is to create a comprehensive online encyclopedia that showcases the rich, diverse, and complex history of South Africa and its people. Whether you are a learner, educator, researcher, or simply curious about our heritage, SAHO provides a trusted resource to explore the stories that shaped our nation.
